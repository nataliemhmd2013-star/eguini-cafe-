Inspired by Khedival Cairo

Opened in 2025 by two friends who wanted a place "like something from the Downtown of the old days," Eugénie takes its name and spirit from the era when Khedive Ismail modelled Downtown on Paris.

ABOUT

The story

According to Scene Eats, Eugénie was launched in April 2025 by Mohamed Hamada and his friend Emad, both with backgrounds in tourism. They took the space without a fixed plan, chasing a vision of Downtown as it was when first built.

The atmosphere

Two rooms with blood-red walls, framed posters of Umm Kulthum, Muhammad Ali and Georgina Rizk, classic songs playing, and outdoor tables on the street. Eugénie's Instagram bio calls it "the beauty of the beauties of the 19th century."

Brand look

Deep red as the lead colour (from the real walls), with warm ivory and brass as supporting design choices.
